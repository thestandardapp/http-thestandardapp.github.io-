How it works

Simple enough
to actually use.

01 — SET UP
Set your standard
Add up to 5 daily goals. Exercise. No alcohol. Read 10 pages. Whatever your standard looks like.
02 — DAILY
Tick your goals
Open the app each day. Tick off what you completed. Hit all your goals and you earn a green dot.
03 — LOCK IN
Lock in the day
Tap Lock In The Day. One tap updates your lock screen wallpaper. Your year fills up dot by dot.
04 — FACE IT
See your year
Every time you pick up your phone you see your entire year. No hiding from it. No excuses.
The philosophy

You either
did it
or you didn't.

Most habit apps bury you in graphs, streaks, and badges. The Standard asks one question every day. Complete your goals and get a green dot. Miss them and get a red dot. 365 dots. One year. Your lock screen holds you accountable so the app doesn't have to.

Your year, visualised

Get started

Up and running
in 5 minutes.

1
Install the Shortcut
This is what updates your lock screen wallpaper automatically. Install it first so it's ready when you need it.
⚡ Install Shortcut
2
Open the app in Safari
Must be opened in Safari on iPhone for full functionality.
🟢 thestandardapp.github.io
3
Add to Home Screen
Tap the Share button in Safari → "Add to Home Screen" → "Add". The Standard now lives on your phone like a real app. Your dots are stored here — don't delete the icon.
4
Set your goals and lock in
Enter your name, add your goals, tap "Set My Standard". Then tick your goals each day and tap "Lock In The Day". Your wallpaper updates instantly.
